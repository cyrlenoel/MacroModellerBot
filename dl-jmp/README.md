# dl-jmp — job market paper: deep-learning global solution of a UK preferred-habitat NK model

This folder supports Cyrille Lenoel's job market paper. The paper builds a nonlinear New Keynesian model with heterogeneous households and a preferred-habitat UK gilt market, where gilt purchases and active QT sales are separate policy tools. The model is solved globally with neural networks. Its parameters are disciplined by IRF targets from high-frequency gilt local projections. The neural-network solution is checked against the linearised solution wherever both apply. The project builds on Kamdar & Ray, "Optimal Unconventional Policy in a New Keynesian Preferred Habitat Model" (CEPR DP 22023, October 2026). There are two fallbacks: a global solution of a sectoral input–output NK model with a binding lower bound, and neural-network estimation of a nonlinear UK model. The review of the core deep-learning methods papers is at [`literature/core-papers-review.md`](literature/core-papers-review.md).

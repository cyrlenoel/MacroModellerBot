# Slide-ready IRFs

PDF and PNG figures for a 16:9 Beamer deck. Generated by `src/plot_slides.py` from the calibrated Python QZ twin (same paths and +100 bp scaling as `src/run_irf.py`). They do not replace the figures in `output/`.

Calibrated MVP, not estimated. The figures have no sterilisation footnote; the slide text carries that explanation.

Agreed shock labels:

- `(a) sterilised term premium` — plotted
- `(b) path news` — plotted
- `(c) sovereign` — not implemented in the MVP. No (c) curve and no (c) figure.

Scenario (a) is a term-premium innovation scaled to +100 bp on the long rate, with an anticipated Bank Rate peg of H = 12 quarters. Scenario (b) is short-rate path news scaled to the same +100 bp long-rate move. H = 12 applies to (a) only. The mortgage-stock lag uses lambda = 0.09.

Regenerate from `uk-long-rate/`:

```bash
python3 src/plot_slides.py
```

## Key numbers from this run

Scenario (a), H = 12, read off the scaled path:

- Long rate on impact, (a): 100.000 annualised bp
- Long rate on impact, (b): 100.000 annualised bp
- Output trough: -0.545% at q4
- House-price trough: -2.597% at q8 (about -2.6% at q8)
- Debt interest IB peak: 0.2017 pp of GDP at q24
- Borrower consumption C^b trough: -1.038% at q9
- Saver consumption C^s trough: -0.768% at q3

## Files

### `a_macro.pdf`, `a_macro.png`

Output, house prices, and debt interest IB under (a) sterilised term premium. The title states +100 bp, anticipated Bank Rate peg H = 12.

### `a_consumption_mortgage.pdf`, `a_consumption_mortgage.png`

Savers C^s against borrowers C^b, next to new-advance R^m against stock R^{m,stock}. The stock lags new advances because lambda = 0.09. H = 12 is in the title.

### `a_vs_b.pdf`, `a_vs_b.png`

Output and house prices. (a) sterilised term premium against (b) path news, both scaled to +100 bp on the long rate. The title states that H = 12 applies to (a) only.

### `ib_decomposition.pdf`, `ib_decomposition.png`

Debt interest IB under (a) sterilised term premium, split into the nominal coupon and the index-linked uplift (including inflation uplift). H = 12.

### `peg_robustness.pdf`, `peg_robustness.png`

The same sterilised term premium at H = 8, 12, 20 and 40. H = 12 is marked as the default. The other peg lengths are robustness cases, not scenario (b) or (c).

### `bank_rate_sterilisation.pdf`, `bank_rate_sterilisation.png`

Bank Rate under (a) sterilised term premium against the Taylor rule free path (same term-premium innovation, peg off), plus the sterilising residual. H = 12.
